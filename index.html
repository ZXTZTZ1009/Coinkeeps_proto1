Financial Mindfulness

What is Saving?

Saving is the practice of setting aside money for future use instead of spending it immediately. It can help a person prepare in case of emergencies, reach financial goals, and build financial stability. According to INDmoney, savings provide a way to set aside money for different financial needs and plans, such as unexpected expenses, long-term plans, and major purchases. Saving regularly also helps develop discipline and responsible financial habits.

Saving means setting aside some of your income for future use. The amount saved can come from income, allowances, or other sources of money. These set aside funds can grow over time and continue to be accessible for different financial requirements.


INDmoney (April, 2025)

Why Should One Budget?

Saving is important because it provides financial security and helps individuals prepare for unexpected expenses and future needs. It can be a backup plan during emergencies and can help prevent relying on others or loans for financial assistance. Saving also allows individuals to work toward important goals and manage their finances more effectively. According to Quanloop, having savings can provide greater financial stability and peace of mind.

Having regular savings also encourages us to practice good financial management. Putting money aside consistently can help individuals keep an emergency fund, prepare for big purchases, and become more financially independent. Saving early and regularly makes it easier to handle unexpected situations and plan for the future. Therefore, saving is an important practice for achieving financial stability and security.


Quanloop Team (n.d.)

How Would One Save?

Saving can begin by setting a budget and carefully tracking your spending. Knowing unnecessary expenses can help reduce spending and allow more money to be set aside. It is also helpful to record your daily spending to determine how much you can save every day. Bank of America recommends including savings as a regular part of the monthly budget and setting specific saving goals. This approach can help develop consistent and responsible financial habits.

Another way to save is by avoiding unnecessary purchases and looking for alternative, less expensive options. Reducing impulsive buying and choosing generic items when necessary can help keep spending under control. Even saving small amounts regularly can gradually build financial security and help achieve future goals.


Bank of America (n.d.)
