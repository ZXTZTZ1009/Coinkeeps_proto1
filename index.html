What is Saving?

Saving is the process of setting aside a portion of your income or allowance instead of spending it immediately. It represents unspent income that is kept for future opportunities or unexpected expenses.

Why Should One Save?

  • Emergency Preparedness: Handles unexpected expenses like medical bills or sudden school needs.
  • Financial Freedom: Reduces reliance on loans or debt.
  • Achieving Goals: Enables major future purchases or investments without financial strain.

How Would One Save?

  • Track Spending: Monitor where every unit of money goes.
  • Set Goals: Aim to save a specific percentage (e.g., 20%) of your income first.
  • Budget Consistently: Distinguish needs from wants and cut unnecessary recurring expenses.
