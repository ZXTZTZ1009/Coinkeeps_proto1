Financial Mindfulness

What is Budgeting?

The process of organizing, planning, and managing your finances by developing a plan is called budgeting. It involves choosing how to spend your money carefully in order to reach those financial goals and keep your expenses from exceeding your income. By following this, individuals, families, and businesses around the world thrive through the ways of wise budgeting.

Why Should One Budget?

Everyone is encouraged to do budgeting. The concept of budgeting can help you in many ways such as helping to save money, keeping expenses in check, planning your weekly spending, and many more.

Budgeting keeps your finances from decreasing before your next paycheck. And through this, it relieves us of stress and worry by providing us peace of mind, knowing where your money goes in your day-to-day life.

How Would One Save?

  • Track Spending: Monitor where every unit of money goes.
  • Set Goals: Aim to save a specific percentage (e.g., 20%) of your income first.
  • Budget Consistently: Distinguish needs from wants and cut unnecessary recurring expenses.
